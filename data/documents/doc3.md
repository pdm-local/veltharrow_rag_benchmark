Veltharrow Comprehensive Zoning Code (VCZC), Consolidated Reference 2024

Veltharrow Comprehensive Zoning Code, enacted by Ordinance Z-1987-001, effective January 1, 1988, and last comprehensively amended by Ordinance Z-2017-009, is hereby set forth as the consolidated zoning reference for the Municipality of Veltharrow. The official zoning map for the municipality is Municipal Zoning Map VTH-ZM-2023, on file with the Director of Planning.

Zoning District Index

The zoning districts of the Municipality of Veltharrow are as follows:

R-1, Low-Density Residential, primary character: single-family detached.

R-2, Medium-Density Residential, primary character: small multifamily.

R-3, High-Density Residential, primary character: apartment and condominium buildings.

C-1, Neighborhood Commercial, primary character: small-scale retail and service.

C-2, General Commercial, primary character: larger retail and office.

C-3, Cornrath Central Business District, primary character: downtown core.

M-1, Light Industrial, primary character: light manufacturing and warehousing.

M-2, Heavy Industrial, primary character: heavy manufacturing, restricted.

PO-1, Lake Orvaine Reach Preservation Overlay, primary character: environmental and mixed-use.

OS-1, Open Space and Parks, primary character: parks, greenways, and conservation.

SP-1, Drasswell Flood Special Purpose, primary character: flood mitigation and restricted development.

Zone R-1 — Low-Density Residential

The R-1 Low-Density Residential District is established for single-family detached residential character. The primary neighborhoods subject to this district include Upper Bryndmere, Gorrath Ridge, and portions of Thornock Rise.

Rule R-1.1 — Minimum Lot Size

The standard minimum lot area in the R-1 District shall be 695 square meters, equal to 0.0695 ha, per dwelling unit.

Exception R-1.1(a): A lot platted before January 1, 1955, may have a minimum lot area as small as 511 square meters, provided that the lot was recorded as a legal conforming lot under the 1948 Charter revision survey.

Exception R-1.1(b): A corner lot shall have a minimum lot area of 743 square meters due to required dual street setbacks.

Rule R-1.2 — Maximum Building Height

No structure in the R-1 District shall exceed 9.5 meters in height, measured from grade to the highest roof point.

Exception R-1.2(a): Accessory structures, including garages and sheds, shall be limited to 4.2 meters in height.

Exception R-1.2(b): Structures located on Gorrath Ridge parcels above 350 meters elevation shall be subject to a reduced maximum height of 8.1 meters pursuant to the Gorrath Ridgeline View Protection Policy, adopted with Ordinance Z-2017-009. The old value for maximum building height in the R-1 zone for Gorrath Ridge parcels above 350 m elevation was 9.5 meters, the same as the standard R-1 maximum. The new value for maximum building height for Gorrath Ridge parcels above 350 m elevation is 8.1 meters.

Rule R-1.3 — Front Setback

The minimum front setback in the R-1 District shall be 7.5 meters from the property line to the primary structure.

Exception R-1.3(a): In Upper Bryndmere, where the average established front setback on a block face exceeds 9.0 meters, new construction shall match the block average rather than the minimum, up to a maximum required setback of 11.0 meters.

Exception R-1.3(b): Covered front porches may encroach up to 2.0 meters into the required front setback, provided that the porch is not enclosed and does not exceed 18 square meters in area.

Rule R-1.4 — Permitted Accessory Dwelling Units

One accessory dwelling unit shall be permitted per lot in the R-1 District, and the maximum floor area of such accessory dwelling unit shall be 74 square meters.

Exception R-1.4(a): Accessory dwelling units located on lots smaller than 600 square meters shall be limited to 55 square meters of floor area.

Exception R-1.4(b): Detached accessory dwelling units shall maintain a 2.5-meter separation from the primary dwelling. Attached accessory dwelling units are exempt from this separation requirement.

Rule R-1.5 — Impervious Surface Coverage

The maximum impervious surface coverage in the R-1 District shall be 38% of lot area. Impervious surfaces include structures, paved areas, and driveways.

Exception R-1.5(a): Permeable pavers and green roof systems shall count at 40% of their actual area toward impervious surface calculations.

Exception R-1.5(b): Lots adjacent to Fellwick Creek, within 30 meters of the centerline, shall have a reduced maximum impervious surface coverage of 27%.

Zone R-2 — Medium-Density Residential

The R-2 Medium-Density Residential District is established for small multifamily residential character. The primary neighborhoods subject to this district include Fellwick South, Drasswell Common, and portions of Cornrath Central.

Rule R-2.1 — Maximum Density

The standard maximum density in the R-2 District shall be 24 dwelling units per hectare of net lot area.

Exception R-2.1(a): Projects that include at least 15% affordable units, defined as affordable to households earning ≤ 60% of municipal median household income, that is, ≤ $35,246 based on 2019 census, may develop at up to 31 dwelling units per hectare. Under Ordinance R-2014-007, the old value, before August 1, 2014, was that affordable units qualified if rents or prices were affordable to households earning ≤ 50% of municipal median household income. The new value, effective August 1, 2014 and current, is that affordable units qualify if rents or prices are affordable to households earning ≤ 60% of municipal median household income, equal to ≤ $35,246 based on the 2019 census figure of $58,743.

Exception R-2.1(b): Senior housing developments, where ≥ 80% of occupants are age 65 or older, may develop at up to 34 dwelling units per hectare.

Rule R-2.2 — Parking Minimums

The standard off-street parking requirement in the R-2 District shall be 1.5 off-street parking spaces per dwelling unit.

Exception R-2.2(a): Buildings within 400 meters of a designated Veltharrow Transit Node shall require only 0.75 spaces per dwelling unit. The designated Veltharrow Transit Nodes are Cornrath Square Station and Drasswell Road Junction.

Exception R-2.2(b): Units with no more than one bedroom shall require only 1.0 space. Studios shall require 0.75 spaces.

Rule R-2.3 — Side Yard Setback

The standard side yard setback in the R-2 District shall be a minimum of 2.1 meters on each side yard.

Exception R-2.3(a): Zero lot-line townhouse developments approved under a Planned Unit Development permit may reduce one side yard to 0.0 meters, provided that the opposite side yard is not less than 3.5 meters.

Exception R-2.3(b): Side yards abutting OS-1 or SP-1 zones shall be increased to 4.5 meters.

Rule R-2.4 — Maximum Building Height

The standard maximum building height in the R-2 District shall be 14.0 meters or 4 stories, whichever is less.

Exception R-2.4(a): The Drasswell Common Neighborhood Plan, adopted November 2021, caps all new residential construction in Drasswell Common at 11.5 meters, or 3 stories, notwithstanding the general R-2 maximum.

Exception R-2.4(b): Elevator mechanical penthouses may extend up to 1.8 meters above the height limit if they cover no more than 20% of the rooftop area.

Zone R-3 — High-Density Residential

The R-3 High-Density Residential District is established for apartment and condominium building character. The primary neighborhoods subject to this district include Pellgate Flats, Wyndmere Spur, and portions of Fellwick South.

Rule R-3.1 — Maximum Density

The standard maximum density in the R-3 District shall be 87 dwelling units per hectare of net lot area.

Exception R-3.1(a): No maximum density applies to projects that dedicate 100% of units as affordable at or below 80% of area median income, subject to Planning Commission approval and execution of a Density Bonus Agreement under Ordinance H-2018-002.

Exception R-3.1(b): Mixed-use buildings with ground-floor commercial space equal to ≥ 40% of the ground floor area may add a density bonus of 15 additional units per hectare above the base maximum.

Rule R-3.2 — Maximum Building Height

The standard maximum building height in the R-3 District shall be 28.0 meters or 8 stories, whichever is less.

Exception R-3.2(a): In Pellgate Flats, the Pellgate Special Height Overlay, established by Ordinance Z-2017-009, Section 14.7, limits all structures to 21.0 meters, or 6 stories, due to aircraft approach path proximity. Under Ordinance Z-2017-009, the old value for maximum building height in R-3 in Pellgate Flats was 28.0 meters, the same as standard R-3. The new value for maximum building height in R-3 in Pellgate Flats under the Pellgate Special Height Overlay is 21.0 meters, or 6 stories, due to aircraft approach path proximity.

Exception R-3.2(b): Rooftop mechanical, stair, and elevator enclosures may extend up to 3.1 meters above the height limit, provided they are set back at least 3.0 meters from all exterior wall planes.

Rule R-3.3 — Ground-Floor Activation

All R-3 buildings fronting on streets designated as Pedestrian Priority Corridors shall have a minimum 65% of ground-floor street frontage devoted to active use. Active use includes retail, lobby, and community space. Active use does not include parking or mechanical space. The Pedestrian Priority Corridors are Cornrath Avenue, Drasswell Road, and Fellwick Lane.

Exception R-3.3(a): Buildings with frontage entirely on side streets, and not on Pedestrian Priority Corridors, are exempt from this requirement.

Exception R-3.3(b): Buildings under 8 units total are exempt from ground-floor activation requirements.

Rule R-3.4 — Open Space Requirement

A minimum of 6.0 square meters of usable open space per dwelling unit shall be provided on-site in the R-3 District. Such usable open space may be rooftop terrace, courtyard, or ground-level open space.

Exception R-3.4(a): Buildings within 150 meters of a publicly accessible OS-1 park of at least 0.4 hectares may reduce on-site open space to 3.5 square meters per unit.

Exception R-3.4(b): Open space on floors above the 5th may not count toward more than 40% of the required open space total.

Zone C-1 — Neighborhood Commercial

The C-1 Neighborhood Commercial District is established for small-scale retail and service character. The primary neighborhoods subject to this district include portions of Thornock Rise and Gorrath Ridge along the Fellwick Creek corridor.

Rule C-1.1 — Maximum Floor Plate

No individual commercial tenant space in the C-1 District shall exceed 465 square meters of gross floor area.

Exception C-1.1(a): Grocery and food retail uses may have a maximum floor plate of 743 square meters.

Exception C-1.1(b): The maximum floor plate limit shall not apply to the ground floor of a mixed-use building where the upper floors are entirely residential.

Rule C-1.2 — Operating Hours

Commercial establishments in the C-1 District may operate between 6:00 AM and 11:00 PM only.

Exception C-1.2(a): Establishments holding a Veltharrow Late-Night Commercial Permit issued under Ordinance C-2003-007 may operate until 1:30 AM.

Exception C-1.2(b): Bakeries and food preparation establishments whose primary business requires early preparation may begin operations at 4:30 AM, provided that no customer-facing operations commence before 6:00 AM.

Rule C-1.3 — Drive-Through Facilities

Drive-through service windows are prohibited in the C-1 District.

Exception C-1.3(a): Pharmacies and financial service establishments, including banks and credit unions, operating drive-throughs as of the effective date of Ordinance Z-2017-009 are legal nonconforming uses and may continue operation indefinitely, but may not expand the drive-through facility.

Exception C-1.3(b): No new drive-through permits shall be issued in the C-1 District under any circumstance.

Zone C-3 — Cornrath Central Business District

The C-3 Cornrath Central Business District is established for downtown core character. The primary neighborhood subject to this district is Cornrath Central.

Rule C-3.1 — Maximum Building Height

The standard maximum building height in the C-3 District shall be 45.0 meters, approximately 13 stories.

Exception C-3.1(a): Properties fronting directly on Cornrath Square, the civic plaza bounded by Cornrath Avenue, Municipal Lane, Quorrath Street, and Civic Drive, shall be subject to a 38.0-meter maximum building height to protect sight lines to the Veltharrow Civic Hall tower.

Exception C-3.1(b): Telecommunications and utility structures may exceed the height maximum by up to 8.5 meters with Planning Commission approval and Council resolution.

Rule C-3.2 — Ground-Floor Commercial Requirement

In the C-3 District, 100% of ground-floor street frontage on Cornrath Avenue and Drasswell Road shall be occupied by active commercial, dining, or civic uses. No residential units, parking entrances, or blank walls are permitted on these frontages at ground level.

Exception C-3.2(a): Lobby entrances for upper-floor office or residential uses may occupy up to 12% of total street frontage width.

Exception C-3.2(b): A property with a legally established residential ground-floor use as of December 31, 2004, prior to Ordinance C-2005-001 coming into effect, may continue as a legal nonconforming use but may not expand the residential footprint.

Ordinance C-2005-001, the Cornrath Central Ground-Floor Commercial Activation Ordinance of 2005, was adopted February 15, 2005, effective July 1, 2005, by Council Vote 9–0, unanimous. The old value, pre-July 2005, was no mandatory ground-floor use requirement; residential and commercial uses were permitted on ground floors in Zone C-3 without restriction. The new value, effective July 1, 2005, is that 100% of ground-floor street frontage on Cornrath Avenue and Drasswell Road must be active commercial, dining, or civic use, and residential units are prohibited at ground floor on these frontages. The grandfathering cutoff date is December 31, 2004. The sponsor was Mayor Colwick Durrath, predecessor to Mayor Ovenspoor, served 2002–2010.

Rule C-3.3 — Minimum Build-To Line

Primary building facades in the C-3 District shall be constructed to within 1.0 meter of the front property line, establishing a near-zero setback requirement to maintain street wall continuity.

Exception C-3.3(a): Outdoor dining areas may be placed in the area between the build-to line and the curb, subject to a Sidewalk Encroachment Permit.

Exception C-3.3(b): Corner properties at Cornrath Square may set back up to 4.0 meters to allow for a widened sidewalk plaza, provided that the space is publicly accessible and maintained by the property owner under a recorded Public Access Easement.

Rule C-3.4 — Signage

The maximum total signage area per building frontage in the C-3 District shall be 0.75 square meters per linear meter of frontage.

Exception C-3.4(a): Internally illuminated signs visible to Cornrath Square shall be limited to a maximum brightness of 350 nits as measured at the sign face.

Exception C-3.4(b): Digital and electronic changeable-copy signs are prohibited in C-3 within 75 meters of the Cornrath Civic Hall. One digital sign per building frontage is permitted elsewhere in C-3, subject to the nit limit above.

Zone M-1 — Light Industrial

The M-1 Light Industrial District is established for light manufacturing, warehousing, assembly, distribution, and related light industrial character. The primary area subject to this district is the southern portion of Drasswell Ward, including the Wyndmere Spur industrial belt.

Rule M-1.1 — Permitted Uses — Exclusions

Light manufacturing, assembly, warehousing, distribution, and accessory office uses are permitted by right in the M-1 District. Retail sales are permitted as an accessory use only and shall not exceed 20% of gross floor area.

Exception M-1.1(a): Retail sales exceeding 20% of gross floor area require a Conditional Use Permit, subject to Planning Commission review and a finding of compatibility with surrounding industrial uses.

Exception M-1.1(b): Residential uses are strictly prohibited in the M-1 District, including caretaker units, except that a single caretaker dwelling unit of no more than 65 square meters is permitted on lots exceeding 2.0 hectares.

Rule M-1.2 — Noise Standards

Sound levels at M-1 property lines shall not exceed 62 decibels, measured as dBA, between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM. Sound levels at M-1 property lines shall not exceed 48 decibels, measured as dBA, between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM.

Exception M-1.2(a): Uses that received Noise Variance Permit NVP-M1-2011-004, Wyndmere Metal Fabricators Ltd., may operate at up to 71 dBA during daytime hours only, under the terms of that permit.

Exception M-1.2(b): Emergency operations of backup generators are exempt from nighttime noise limits for up to 4 consecutive hours per event.

Rule M-1.3 — Landscaping Buffer

A minimum 8.5-meter landscaped buffer with continuous tree planting shall be required along all M-1 property lines that abut a residential zone. Continuous tree planting shall consist of one canopy tree per 6 linear meters of frontage.

Exception M-1.3(a): Where a public alley or road right-of-way of at least 9.0 meters in width separates the M-1 lot from the residential zone, the buffer may be reduced to 4.0 meters.

Exception M-1.3(b): The landscaping buffer requirement does not apply to M-1 property lines abutting other M-1 or M-2 zones.

Zone PO-1 — Lake Orvaine Reach Preservation Overlay

The PO-1 Lake Orvaine Reach Preservation Overlay is established for environmental and mixed-use character. The primary neighborhood subject to this overlay is Lake Orvaine Reach.

Rule PO-1.1 — Shoreline Setback

No structure shall be constructed within 38.5 meters of the ordinary high-water mark of Lake Orvaine.

Exception PO-1.1(a): Water-dependent structures, including docks, boat launches, and pump stations, may be placed within the shoreline setback subject to Provincial Environmental Authority permit and Veltharrow Shoreline Development Permit.

Exception PO-1.1(b): Restoration or replacement of legally existing structures within the shoreline setback that were damaged by a declared natural disaster may proceed, provided that the replacement footprint does not exceed the original footprint.

Under Ordinance Z-2017-009, the old value for shoreline setback in the then-unoverlaid residential zone was 25.0 meters from ordinary high-water mark. The new value for shoreline setback in Zone PO-1 is 38.5 meters from ordinary high-water mark.

Rule PO-1.2 — Impervious Surface Cap

Total impervious surface in Zone PO-1 shall not exceed 22% of any individual lot area.

Exception PO-1.2(a): Stormwater retention or infiltration features certified by a licensed engineer to retain the first 38 millimeters of any rainfall event on-site may be credited at 50% toward impervious surface calculations.

Exception PO-1.2(b): Existing properties exceeding the 22% impervious surface cap as of January 1, 2018, the date of overlay adoption, are legal nonconforming. Such properties may not increase impervious coverage further, but may replace existing impervious surfaces in kind.

Rule PO-1.3 — Vegetation Removal

No trees with a diameter at breast height, DBH, of 15 centimeters or greater may be removed within Zone PO-1 without a Veltharrow Tree Removal Permit.

Exception PO-1.3(a): Dead, dying, or hazardous trees as certified by a licensed arborist may be removed without a permit, provided that the landowner retains documentation.

Exception PO-1.3(b): Invasive species identified on the Veltharrow Invasive Plant Registry, maintained by the Director of Planning, may be removed without a permit.

Zone SP-1 — Drasswell Flood Special Purpose

The SP-1 Drasswell Flood Special Purpose District is established for flood mitigation and restricted development. The primary neighborhood subject to this district is portions of Drasswell Common near Drasswell Pond.

Rule SP-1.1 — Development Prohibition

No new habitable structure shall be constructed within Zone SP-1.

Exception SP-1.1(a): Public infrastructure, including flood control structures, pump stations, and stormwater management facilities, may be constructed with approval of the Director of Public Works and Planning Commission.

Exception SP-1.1(b): Temporary structures associated with emergency flood response may be placed without a permit for up to 90 days per declared event.

Rule SP-1.2 — Flood Mitigation Requirements for Adjacent Properties

Any new construction on properties within 55 meters of a SP-1 zone boundary shall elevate the lowest habitable floor to at least 0.8 meters above the 100-year flood elevation as established by the Orvaine Provincial Flood Authority's 2016 Floodplain Mapping. The base flood elevation for Drasswell Common is 97.4 meters AMSL. The minimum habitable floor is 98.2 meters AMSL.

Exception SP-1.2(a): Accessory structures under 14 square meters are exempt from elevation requirements.

Exception SP-1.2(b): Properties that have completed the Drasswell Common Flood Proofing Certification Program, administered under Ordinance SP-2019-001, and hold a valid Flood Proofing Certificate are exempt from per-project elevation review, provided that their certificate is current. Flood Proofing Certificates are renewable every 7 years.

Ordinance SP-2019-001, the Drasswell Common Flood Proofing Certification Program Ordinance of 2019, was adopted April 30, 2019, effective September 1, 2019, by Council Vote 7–2 in favor. The old value, pre-September 2019, was that Flood Proofing Certificates under the predecessor administrative policy were renewed every 5 years. The new value, effective September 1, 2019 and current, is that Flood Proofing Certificates are renewed every 7 years. The administering office is the Director of Public Works, Cael Drossmund. The minimum base flood elevation standard also codified by this ordinance is lowest habitable floor equals base flood elevation, 97.4 m AMSL, plus 0.8 m, equal to 98.2 m AMSL. This standard had previously been set at base flood elevation plus 0.5 m, equal to 97.9 m AMSL, under the 2012 administrative policy.

Rule SP-1.3 — Vegetation Maintenance

All non-paved areas within 12 meters of Drasswell Pond's ordinary high-water mark shall be maintained with native, deep-rooted vegetation consistent with the Veltharrow Shoreline Vegetation Standards, VSVS-2017.

Exception SP-1.3(a): A permitted maintenance access path of no more than 2.0 meters in width is allowed.

Exception SP-1.3(b): Public recreational areas designated under OS-1 overlay within SP-1 may include mown grass areas not exceeding 40% of the designated recreation parcel.

General Codified References Applicable to Zoning Administration

The Veltharrow Comprehensive Zoning Code is administered by the municipal planning authority by reference to Municipal Zoning Map VTH-ZM-2023, on file with the Director of Planning. The Director of Planning is Ysabel Frondtke, with term start February 1, 2021, by civil service appointment for an indefinite career term. The Veltharrow Planning Commission has 7 members, appointed by Council, with 3-year staggered terms. Ysabel Frondtke serves as Chair ex officio and non-voting. Dunstan Worrall serves as Vice-Chair, appointed, with term ending March 2025.

Where this Code refers to the Veltharrow Municipal Council, the governing body is the Veltharrow Municipal Council. The Council Type is Mayor–Council, Strong Mayor form. The number of Council seats is 9, consisting of 1 Mayor at-large and 8 Ward Councillors, 2 per ward. The number of wards is 4, being Cornrath Ward, Fellwick Ward, Gorrath Ward, and Drasswell Ward. The Council meeting location is Veltharrow Civic Hall, Council Chamber B. Regular meetings are held on the first and third Tuesday of each month at 6:30 PM. The Council term length is 4 years, staggered. Municipal elections are held every 2 years, alternating ward pairs. The next scheduled election is October 2026.

Where this Code refers to Council resolution, Planning Commission approval, a Planned Unit Development permit, a Conditional Use Permit, a Sidewalk Encroachment Permit, a Public Access Easement, a Veltharrow Shoreline Development Permit, a Veltharrow Tree Removal Permit, a Veltharrow Late-Night Commercial Permit, a Flood Proofing Certificate, or a Density Bonus Agreement, each such instrument shall be applied according to the exact conditions stated in the district rule and exception in which the instrument appears.

Where this Code refers to Ordinance Z-2017-009, the full name is Veltharrow Comprehensive Zoning Amendment Ordinance of 2017. It was adopted October 3, 2017, effective January 1, 2018, by Council Vote 5–4 in favor, the narrowest margin of any included ordinance. Its subject was an omnibus zoning amendment. For Gorrath Ridge maximum building height, the old value was maximum building height in R-1 zone for Gorrath Ridge parcels above 350 m elevation equal to 9.5 meters, the same as standard R-1, and the new value was maximum building height for Gorrath Ridge parcels above 350 m elevation equal to 8.1 meters under the Gorrath Ridgeline View Protection Policy. For Lake Orvaine Reach shoreline setback, the old value was shoreline setback in the then-unoverlaid residential zone equal to 25.0 meters from ordinary high-water mark, and the new value was shoreline setback in Zone PO-1 equal to 38.5 meters from ordinary high-water mark. For the Pellgate Flats Height Overlay, the old value was maximum building height in R-3 in Pellgate Flats equal to 28.0 meters, the same as standard R-3, and the new value was maximum building height in R-3 in Pellgate Flats under the Pellgate Special Height Overlay equal to 21.0 meters, or 6 stories, due to aircraft approach path proximity. The dissenting votes were Councillors Rund Pallister, Prinna Valcourt, Marthyl Dunnegan, and Oresten Bluffe.

Where this Code refers to Ordinance R-2014-007, the full name is Veltharrow Residential Density Bonus Ordinance of 2014. It was adopted June 17, 2014, effective August 1, 2014, by Council Vote 8–1 in favor. Its subject was changing the affordable housing income threshold used to qualify for density bonuses in Zone R-2. The old value, pre-August 2014, was that affordable units qualified if rents or prices were affordable to households earning ≤ 50% of municipal median household income. The new value, effective August 1, 2014 and current, is that affordable units qualify if rents or prices are affordable to households earning ≤ 60% of municipal median household income, equal to ≤ $35,246 based on 2019 census figure of $58,743. The sponsor was Councillor Emmelyn Foscroft, Drasswell Ward, Seat 4B.

Where this Code refers to Ordinance C-2005-001, the full name is Cornrath Central Ground-Floor Commercial Activation Ordinance of 2005. It was adopted February 15, 2005, effective July 1, 2005, by Council Vote 9–0, unanimous. Its subject was establishing mandatory ground-floor commercial use along Cornrath Avenue and Drasswell Road in Zone C-3. The old value, pre-July 2005, was no mandatory ground-floor use requirement, with residential and commercial uses permitted on ground floors in Zone C-3 without restriction. The new value, effective July 1, 2005, was that 100% of ground-floor street frontage on Cornrath Avenue and Drasswell Road must be active commercial, dining, or civic use, and residential units are prohibited at ground floor on these frontages. The grandfathering cutoff date is December 31, 2004. The sponsor was Mayor Colwick Durrath, predecessor to Mayor Ovenspoor, served 2002–2010.

Where this Code refers to Ordinance SP-2019-001, the full name is Drasswell Common Flood Proofing Certification Program Ordinance of 2019. It was adopted April 30, 2019, effective September 1, 2019, by Council Vote 7–2 in favor. Its subject was establishing the Drasswell Common Flood Proofing Certification Program and changing the renewal period for flood proofing certificates. The old value, pre-September 2019, was Flood Proofing Certificates under predecessor administrative policy renewed every 5 years. The new value, effective September 1, 2019 and current, is Flood Proofing Certificates renewed every 7 years. The administering office is Director of Public Works, Cael Drossmund. The minimum base flood elevation standard also codified by this ordinance is lowest habitable floor equal to base flood elevation, 97.4 m AMSL, plus 0.8 m, equal to 98.2 m AMSL. This standard had previously been set at base flood elevation plus 0.5 m, equal to 97.9 m AMSL, under the 2012 administrative policy.
