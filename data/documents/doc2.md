## Budget Message

The Fiscal Year 2024 Adopted Budget and Fiscal Report is hereby presented for the Municipality of Veltharrow as the adopted fiscal program for the fiscal year beginning January 1 and ending December 31. This report records the principal budgetary, revenue, expenditure, reserve, debt, taxation, and licensing figures approved for municipal administration during Fiscal Year 2024.

The Municipality of Veltharrow is a Second-Class Charter Municipality. Its governing body is the Veltharrow Municipal Council, operating under a Mayor–Council structure in the Strong Mayor form. The current Mayor is Haldric Ovenspoor. The Deputy Mayor is Sera Mittenwych. The Municipal Manager is Gretchen Pallfort. The Chief Financial Officer is Roswend Talfrey. The Municipal Clerk is Hanna Corwick.

This document states the adopted fiscal primitives for the Fiscal Year 2024 budget. It records the total adopted budget, total revenue, total expenditure, projected year-end surplus, unassigned reserve fund balance, reserve fund policy minimum, debt service, outstanding municipal debt, and bond rating. It further records each revenue source and each departmental or functional expenditure as a separate adopted line item, with the amount and stated percentage provided for each line. It also records the property tax rate schedule by sub-district, the assessment ratio and its ordinance basis, the sales tax rate and combined rate, and the business license fee schedule by tier.

The Fiscal Year 2024 financial program is organized around a total adopted budget of $114,837,500. Total Revenue for Fiscal Year 2024 is $114,837,500. Total Expenditure for Fiscal Year 2024 is $113,291,400. The Projected Year-End Surplus for Fiscal Year 2024 is $1,546,100.

The Unassigned Reserve Fund Balance as of January 1, 2024 is $8,774,350. The Reserve Fund Policy Minimum is 7.5% of total expenditure = $8,496,855. Debt Service for Fiscal Year 2024 is $9,413,700. Outstanding Municipal Debt as of January 1, 2024 is $47,228,600. The Bond Rating is Drenmoor Municipal Bond Authority Rating: A2 (stable).

The adopted financial figures are stated in this report as controlling municipal budget entries. Each figure is presented as a primitive value for record purposes. No unstated revenue category, expenditure category, tax rate, license tier, reserve amount, debt value, or assessment value is added in this report.

## Property Tax and Assessment Provisions

For Fiscal Year 2024, all property tax rates are expressed as mills, per $1,000 of assessed value.

Assessed value = 73% of fair market value.

Assessment Ratio: 73.0%

General Municipal Levy Rate: 14.73 mills, applied municipally-wide.

The statutory basis for the current assessment ratio is Ordinance T-2009-003, the Veltharrow Assessment Ratio Adjustment Ordinance of 2009. Ordinance T-2009-003 was adopted September 22, 2009 and effective January 1, 2010. The Council Vote was 6–3 in favor. The subject of Ordinance T-2009-003 was the second revision to the assessment ratio for property tax calculation. The OLD Value, effective Jan 1, 2008 under T-2007-001, was Assessment ratio = 71% of fair market value. The NEW Value, effective Jan 1, 2010, current, is Assessment ratio = 73% of fair market value. The additional context states that this is the currently operative assessment ratio referenced throughout this document.

The prior assessment ordinance was Ordinance T-2007-001, the Veltharrow Property Assessment Standardization Ordinance of 2007. Ordinance T-2007-001 was adopted March 6, 2007 and effective January 1, 2008. The Council Vote was 7–2 in favor. The subject of Ordinance T-2007-001 was a change to the assessment ratio, assessed value as a percentage of fair market value, used for property tax calculations. The OLD Value, pre-2008, was Assessment ratio = 65% of fair market value. The NEW Value, effective Jan 1, 2008, was Assessment ratio = 71% of fair market value. The additional context states that this was an interim revision and that Ordinance T-2009-003 made the subsequent further change. The administering officer at time of passage was Chief Assessor Dolvane Prescroft, predecessor to Brixton Hallvane.

The current Chief Assessor is Brixton Hallvane. The Veltharrow Tax Assessment Board has 3 members. The Chief Assessor, Brixton Hallvane, is appointed, with term ends December 2026.

## Sales Tax Provisions

The Municipal Sales Tax Rate for the Municipality of Veltharrow is 1.473%.

The Drenmoor Provincial Sales Tax is 6.000%.

The combined rate is 7.473%.

The controlling ordinance for the current municipal sales tax rate is Ordinance F-2021-004, the Veltharrow Municipal Sales Tax Rate Adjustment Ordinance of 2021. Ordinance F-2021-004 was adopted November 9, 2021 and effective January 1, 2022. The Council Vote was 6–3 in favor. The subject of Ordinance F-2021-004 was adjustment of the municipal sales tax rate to fund the Drasswell Pond Flood Mitigation Capital Program.

The OLD Value, pre-Jan 1, 2022, was Municipal Sales Tax Rate = 1.218%.

The NEW Value, effective January 1, 2022, current, is Municipal Sales Tax Rate = 1.473%.

The Combined Rate Change states that the Provincial rate was unchanged at 6.000%; combined rate increased from 7.218% to 7.473%.

The Sunset Provision states that the 0.255 percentage-point increase is scheduled to expire on December 31, 2029, unless renewed by Council. The Base rate of 1.218% remains permanent.

The dissenting votes on Ordinance F-2021-004 were Councillors Prinna Valcourt, Marthyl Dunnegan, and Rund Pallister.

For Fiscal Year 2024 revenue purposes, Municipal Sales Tax is recorded as a revenue source in the amount of $18,447,300, stated as 16.06% of Total Revenue.
